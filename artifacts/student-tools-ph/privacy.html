Student Tools PH is designed to work without an account. Your study data belongs to you.

PRIVACY POLICYLast updated: 23 October 2026

What stays on your device

Your grade and GWA calculations are performed in your browser. Study planner tasks are saved in your browser’s local storage so they’re still here when you return on the same device and browser.

We do not send your grades, subjects, units, timer activity, or planner entries to a server. We do not require you to create an account.

Theme preference

If you choose light or dark mode, that preference is also saved in your browser’s local storage. It is used only to remember your choice on this device.

Your control

You can remove planner entries from the app at any time. You can also clear this site’s browser storage through your browser settings; doing so removes saved planner tasks and your theme preference.

Saved information is specific to the browser and device you used. It does not sync across devices, and clearing browser data may remove it.

Changes to this page

If the way this tool handles information changes, this page will be updated. The current version is dated 23 October 2026.

In short: Your study data stays in your browser on your device. It is not sent to us.
